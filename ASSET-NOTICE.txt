The PNG files in this directory are American woodcock pixel-art sources created
by the project owner. The first file, woodcock-base.png, is the fixed lead
individual. The other six files are used for later flock members.

The runtime preserves the supplied transparent pixels and uses the remaining
pixels as the desktop characters. Before publishing a public binary that
includes these assets, confirm that the project owner has granted
redistribution and modification permission for the images. The source-code
MIT license does not automatically change the image rights.

The project-level reuse policy is documented in TERMS.md. It allows
commercial use, modification, public release, and redistribution of the
supplied images without a required credit, but only to the extent that the
project owner controls or has permission for the relevant rights. Third-party
image-generation or source-material terms still apply.
